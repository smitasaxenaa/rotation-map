Rotation Map / NSE industries

Where capital is winning and losing, measured against the CNX500.
Sample data These numbers are synthetic, generated for layout. Run python build_data.py to replace data.json with live TradingView figures.
How each column is calculated
Weight
Industry market cap as a share of the whole universe. Tells you whether a move is meaningful or a rounding error.
Mood
A −6 to +6 score blending 3M and 1M relative strength, the share of members above their 50- and 200-day averages, and distance from the 52-week high. Bullish at +3, bearish at −2.
Cycle
Position on a relative-strength lifecycle. Lagging: losing on 3M and 1M. Basing: 3M still negative, 1M turned up. Early: 3M positive, 1Y not yet. Mid: 3M, 6M and 1Y all positive. Late: over a year of outperformance, pinned within 8% of the 52-week high, and 1M decelerating.
Trend
One year of relative strength — the industry divided by the CNX500, indexed to 100. Reconstructed from the horizon returns, so no price history is stored.
52W
Weighted distance below the 52-week high. 0.0% means the industry is sitting at its high.
Returns
Capped market-cap weighted. No single stock can exceed 25% of its industry, so one giant name can't masquerade as a whole industry.
vs CNX500
Return compounded against the benchmark, not subtracted from it: (1+industry) ÷ (1+index) − 1. Over 3 months the two agree; over 5 years subtraction gives numbers like −180%, which mean nothing. The Absolute / vs CNX500 toggle re-bases every return column.
Breadth
Share of members trading above their 200-day average. A rally with low breadth is two stocks, not a rotation.
Turnover
Today's traded value divided by the 10-day average. A rough participation check, not a measure of fund flows.
Star stock
Beats its own industry over 3M, trades above its 200-day average, and is within 15% of its 52-week high.
Relative performance is not fund flow. This shows which industries are outperforming the index and how broadly — it does not observe money entering or leaving anything. Nothing here is investment advice; it is a screening and journaling instrument.
